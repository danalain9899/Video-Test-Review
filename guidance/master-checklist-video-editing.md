# Master Checklist: Video Editing (Talking Head, Single Camera, 1 Shot)

Konsep: satu rakaman utama + lapisan visual + audio + teknik editing = video lebih dinamik dan menarik.
Ini library pilihan, BUKAN senarai wajib. Setiap effect mesti ada tujuan: menerangkan, menekankan, membina mood, atau mengekalkan perhatian.

## 1. Visual Movement & Camera Effects (elak visual statik)
Zoom In/Out · Punch-In/Out (tiba-tiba pada point penting) · Digital Pan & Tilt · Crop & Reframe (wide→medium→close-up) · Speed Change · Freeze Frame · Shake Effect (terkawal) · Motion Blur

## 2. B-Roll, Overlay & Visual Inserts (variasi + bantu faham)
B-Roll · Image Overlay · Screen Recording · Picture-in-Picture · Screenshot Overlay (komen/review/data/bukti) · Split Screen · Cutaway · Background Replacement (masking/green screen) · Before & After · Blur/Highlight Area

## 3. Text, Captions & Motion Graphics (mesej jelas, mudah diingati)
Auto Captions (betulkan transkripsi) · Animated Captions · Keyword Highlight · Text Overlay · Callout & Arrow · Lower Third · Animated Icons · Motion Graphics · Counters & Statistics · Progress Bar · Title Cards · Logo & Branding (konsisten)

## 4. Audio, Music & SFX (kualiti suara, mood, impak)
BGM (ikut mood/topik/tempo) · SFX · Whoosh (transisi) · Pop/Click/Ding (teks, ikon, idea) · Impact/Hit · Riser/Build-Up · Audio Ducking (turunkan muzik bila bercakap) · Noise Reduction · EQ & Compression · Voice-over · Ambient Sound · Silence/Audio Drop · Beat Sync · J-Cut/L-Cut

## 5. Transitions, Effects & Visual Polish
Hard Cut · Jump Cut (buang jeda) · Fade In/Out · Zoom Transition · Whip Transition · Match Cut · Masking/Rotoscoping · Tracking · Color Correction (exposure, WB) · Color Grading (mood) · Glow/Shadow/Light Leak · Glitch/Distortion · Film Grain · Background Blur · Parallax

## 6. Pacing, Storytelling & Audience Retention (tentukan bila & kenapa guna elemen lain)
Hook · Jump-Cut Pacing (buang pause/kesilapan/ulangan) · Pattern Interrupt · Visual Variation (talking head ↔ zoom ↔ grafik ↔ gambar ↔ B-roll) · Emphasis Timing · Curiosity Gap · Build-Up & Reveal · Problem → Solution · Storytelling · Pacing Control · Payoff (tunaikan janji hook) · CTA · Ending & Loop · Audience Retention Review

## Workflow setiap video
1. Skrip: hook, isi utama, contoh, penekanan, CTA
2. Storyboard: tanda bahagian untuk zoom, B-roll, overlay, grafik, SFX, perubahan pacing
3. Rakaman satu shot, kamera kekal satu angle
4. Editing: gabung teknik relevan dari 6 kategori
5. Quality check sebelum eksport: suara, subtitle, pacing, ketepatan visual, keterbacaan, CTA

## Prinsip penting
- Pilih yang relevan sahaja; kejelasan, pacing dan storytelling lebih penting daripada banyak effect.
- Digital zoom/crop kurangkan kualiti jika resolusi asal rendah.
- B-roll boleh ditambah masa editing tanpa tukar angle rakaman.
